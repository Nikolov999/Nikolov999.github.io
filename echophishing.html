EchoPhishing — local email monitoring + phishing analysis.

EchoPhishing is a desktop application that connects to your mailbox via IMAP (with your permission), monitors incoming messages while active, and flags suspicious patterns using multiple indicators — including header signals (SPF/DKIM/DMARC results), link/brand deception patterns, and common social-engineering tactics. Everything runs on your device.

Privacy & control first

EchoPhishing is designed to run locally. Email analysis, scan history, and generated reports remain on your PC. You control what gets stored and what gets exported.

Real-time mailbox monitoring (IMAP)

When enabled, EchoPhishing watches new emails as they arrive and scores them based on multiple risk indicators.

  • • Connect one or multiple mailboxes (client-controlled)
  • • Flags suspicious messages early (before users interact)
  • • Stores scan results locally for review
  • • Designed for triage and awareness — not “magic detection”

Manual analysis tab (paste raw email)

For deeper checks, analysts can paste raw email content/headers to inspect authentication and deception signals.

  • • Parses headers and authentication results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)
  • • Highlights risky link patterns and lookalike domains
  • • Extracts indicators for investigation workflow
  • • Helps document “why” an email is risky (not just a score)

Dashboard & history

A central dashboard shows past analyses and monitoring events so teams can track what’s happening over time.

  • • View all scans in one place
  • • Filter by scan type (monitoring vs manual analysis)
  • • Sort by risk level, time, sender, or mailbox
  • • Useful for recurring campaigns and trend spotting

Optional reporting

Generate a clean report when you need a shareable summary for internal IT, management, or an incident timeline.

  • • One-click export for selected items
  • • Clear explanations + recommended next steps
  • • Built for owners/managers (not just technical staff)
  • • Keeps evidence organised during an incident

How EchoPhishing fits into a real workflow

Most phishing failures aren’t because people are stupid — it’s because there’s no fast, consistent process to evaluate suspicious messages. EchoPhishing is built to support a simple workflow: monitor → triage → investigate → document.

1) Connect mailbox (opt-in). Configure IMAP for one or more addresses you control.
2) Monitor while active. New emails are evaluated and logged locally as they arrive.
3) Deep-dive when needed. Paste raw headers/content for a detailed breakdown of indicators.
4) Review in dashboard. Filter by monitoring events vs manual scans and track repeated patterns.
5) Export a report (optional). Share findings with IT/management or attach to an incident record.

Important: Email authentication signals (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and “passing” checks are not a guarantee of safety. EchoPhishing uses multiple indicators to support decision-making — it doesn’t pretend to be a perfect oracle.
