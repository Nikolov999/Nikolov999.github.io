EchoScanner: fast discovery that supports real pentesting

Tools don’t replace manual testing — they speed up the boring parts so the effort goes into validation and impact. EchoPentest uses two layers: a public “Lite” scanner for safe reconnaissance, and an internal NextGen workflow used only in authorized engagements.

EchoScanner Lite (public)

A safe, lightweight checker for early visibility. It helps you understand what your domain publicly exposes at a metadata level — without scanning services or attempting access.

  • DNS records: A / AAAA / MX / NS / TXT / SOA
  • WHOIS snapshot: registrar + key dates + name servers (when available)
  • Useful context: helps scope an engagement and catch obvious configuration mistakes early

  1. Discovery call — 20–30 minutes to understand your environment and drivers.

Documentation & legal coverage you can show to anyone

Every engagement includes the paperwork and structure needed to keep management, clients and insurers comfortable.

Scope of Work (SoW)

  • Defines exactly which systems, apps and ranges are in scope.
  • Outlines what is out of scope to reduce surprises.
  • Documents timelines, objectives and deliverables.

Rules of Engagement (RoE)

  • Allowed techniques, tools and intensity levels.
  • Operational safety and change-control expectations.
  • Emergency contacts and escalation paths.

Authorization to Test

  • Formal written approval from an authorized decision maker.
  • Clarifies testing is intentional, agreed and legal.
  • Protects your business if questions arise later.

Final Reporting Package

  • Executive summary for management and external stakeholders.
  • Technical findings with evidence, risk rating and remediation steps.
  • Optional retest + updated report after fixes.
